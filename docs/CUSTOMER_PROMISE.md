# Customer Promise

**Status: Permanent governing document.** Subordinate to `COMPANY_CONSTITUTION.md`. This document describes what any customer of any Metrix ecosystem product can rely on.

## What Customers Can Expect

- Every price, trial length, and billing term stated to you is true and matches what you'll actually be charged (currently: Command Center $99/month, Founder CRM $39/month with founder-code eligibility, both with a 7-day free trial, billed monthly, cancel anytime).
- Every feature described as available is actually shipped and working — not a roadmap item presented as live.
- Your customer, property, and business data belongs to you.

## What We Will Never Do

- We will never let AI take an action — sending a message, changing a record, contacting your customer — without your explicit approval first.
- We will never fabricate reviews, testimonials, user counts, or results to make the product look more proven than it is.
- We will never use fake urgency or scarcity tactics to pressure a purchase decision.
- We will never bury cancellation or refund terms — see `MCC_BILLING_TERMS` on the pricing page for the exact, current policy.
- We will never present a roadmap feature (e.g., SMS/social messaging, currently in development) as available today.

## Privacy

Your account is workspace-isolated with secure authentication. What data is collected and why is documented in the Privacy Policy (`/privacy`), which is kept accurate as new data-collection points (such as the landing-page lead form) are added — not left describing an outdated version of the product.

## Trust

Trust is earned through verifiable behavior, not claimed through marketing copy: a full audit trail of every AI recommendation and every decision, an instant kill switch on any automation, and workspace-level data isolation.

## Transparency

Where something isn't ready yet, we say so plainly. Where a claim can't be backed by real, current product behavior, it doesn't get published. This applies equally to marketing pages and to internal reporting on our own progress.

## Human Approval

This is the architectural center of the product, not a marketing line: no AI-drafted recommendation in Metrix Command Center reaches a customer or takes an action until a human reviews and approves it. There is no autonomous mode.

## Honest AI

Buster and every other AI capability in the product are built to recommend based on your real data — never to fabricate a plausible-sounding but ungrounded suggestion. If the AI doesn't have enough real evidence to make a confident recommendation, it says so rather than guessing.

## Product Quality

Customer-facing work is held to a premium bar — visually, technically, and in copy — regardless of how early-stage the company is. Being early doesn't excuse being sloppy.

## Support Philosophy

Direct and honest: reach us at info@subzerometrix.com and we will respond. No support theater, no scripted deflection — real answers from a small, accountable team.

## Long-Term Commitment

This product is built by an operator who spent 24+ years inside the exact industry it serves, alongside a family that still works in it today. The commitment to this product is not a side project — it is the direct continuation of that operational background, and the company's principles (truth over hype, human approval, evidence over assertion) are the same standards the founder has applied to real facilities and mechanical systems for two decades.
