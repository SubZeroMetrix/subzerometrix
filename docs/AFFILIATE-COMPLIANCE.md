# Affiliate Compliance

## Disclosure Requirements

Affiliate disclosures must appear:
- Near affiliate CTAs
- Before commercial comparison content
- On review pages
- On product pages
- In the dedicated /affiliate-disclosure page
- In the footer (link to full disclosure)

## Standard Disclosure Text

"SubZero Metrix may earn a commission if you sign up through links on this page. This does not affect our editorial recommendations."

## Editorial Independence

- Recommendations are based on use-case fit, not commission rates
- Products without affiliate programs are included when relevant
- Commission rates do not determine ranking or editorial scores
- No payment is accepted for favorable reviews

## Approval Workflow

1. Identify affiliate program for product
2. Apply to affiliate program
3. Receive approval
4. Add affiliate link to database with `approval_status: 'approved'`
5. Set `is_active: true` only after approval
6. Record commission terms as display text (not exact figures unless verified)

## Verification Process

- Product information verified against vendor-published sources
- Each listing includes last-verified date
- Commission claims only published if verified from authoritative sources
- Features and pricing checked against official documentation

## Prohibited Practices

- Do not fabricate commission rates or earnings
- Do not imply partnership or endorsement unless accurate
- Do not use vendor logos without trademark permission
- Do not create fake urgency, scarcity, or discounts
- Do not classify clicks as sales or revenue
