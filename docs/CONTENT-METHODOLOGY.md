# Content Methodology

## Evaluation Criteria

Every product listed on SubZero Metrix is evaluated on:

1. **Use-case fit** — Who is this tool actually built for? Who should avoid it?
2. **Features** — What does it do well? What are the core capabilities?
3. **Limitations** — Where does it fall short? What are the honest drawbacks?
4. **Pricing** — What does it cost? Is there a free plan or trial?
5. **Complexity** — How difficult is it to set up and use?
6. **Verification** — Are the claimed features confirmed from vendor sources?

## Scoring

Editorial scores (when applied) are based on the criteria above. Scores are editorial assessments, not statistical measurements.

## Verification

- Product details are verified against vendor websites, documentation, and pricing pages
- Each listing includes a last-verified date
- Verification records are maintained in the database
- Corrections are made promptly when reported

## Comparisons

Comparison pages include:
- Best-by-use-case recommendations
- Side-by-side feature tables
- Pricing comparison
- Strengths of each option
- Limitations of each option
- Honest assessment of who should choose which

## Tool Finder

The Tool Finder uses deterministic rule logic. Recommendations are based on:
- User's stated goal
- Current stage
- First priority
- Budget
- Technical level

No AI-generated recommendations are used at launch. All recommendation rules are explicit and auditable.

## Content Quality

- No fabricated reviews or testimonials
- No fake user counts or social proof
- No guaranteed income or results claims
- No keyword-stuffed or thin content pages
- No mass-generated AI content without editorial review
