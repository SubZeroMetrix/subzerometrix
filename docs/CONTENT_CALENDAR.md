# 12-Month Content Calendar

*See [`SEO_CONTENT_ARCHITECTURE.md`](./SEO_CONTENT_ARCHITECTURE.md) for the pillars/clusters this calendar publishes into. Governed by [`PRODUCT_PHILOSOPHY.md`](./PRODUCT_PHILOSOPHY.md)'s evidence-over-hype principle — every planned piece must be genuinely useful and honest, not SEO filler. Planning only — no pages built this pass.*

Prioritization rule: highest-ROI topics first, defined as (a) real founder expertise (HVAC, Facility Management, Mechanical — see `FOUNDER_PROFILE.md`) combined with (b) direct product relevance (AI for Contractors, Business Operations). Trades where the founder has no direct lived experience (Electrical, Plumbing as primary focus) are sequenced later and would benefit from real subject-matter input before publishing, consistent with the no-fabrication standard — do not publish generic trade content that reads as written by someone without real knowledge of the trade.

## Launch Window (pre-Day 1 — resource center foundation)

- Resource hub page + navigation structure.
- 1 pillar page live at launch: **AI for Contractors** (highest product relevance, zero trade-specific authenticity risk since it's about the product's own domain).
- 2-3 supporting pieces under it: "What governed AI actually means," "Human-approval AI vs. autonomous AI," "AI adoption checklist for trade businesses."

## First 90 Days

**Month 1**
- HVAC pillar page (founder's strongest authenticity asset) + 3 cluster pieces: seasonal maintenance scheduling, missed-follow-up cost calculator (build as a real interactive tool, not a static article), technician dispatch basics.
- Facility Management pillar page (direct founder background match) + 2 cluster pieces: multi-site PM program structure, BAS/BMS basics.

**Month 2**
- Business Operations pillar page + 3 cluster pieces: CRM basics for service businesses, revenue-recovery fundamentals, follow-up process design.
- Mechanical pillar page (adjacent to founder's facilities background) + 2 cluster pieces.
- First calculator tool: missed-follow-up revenue impact (real, functional, honest — no fabricated benchmark numbers, user-input-driven only).

**Month 3**
- Commercial Service pillar page + 2 cluster pieces.
- First downloadable checklist/template (e.g., a seasonal maintenance checklist, a new-customer onboarding SOP template) — genuinely useful, not a lead-gen gimmick with thin content behind it.
- 90-day retrospective: review real traffic/engagement data (once Analytics has meaningful volume) and adjust remaining calendar — per `PRODUCT_PHILOSOPHY.md`'s continuous-improvement principle, not guessed adjustments.

## Months 4-6

- Electrical and Plumbing pillar pages — sequenced later specifically because they need real subject-matter input (interview a real electrician/plumber, or partner content) rather than generic AI-written trade content.
- Expand AI for Contractors cluster with real product-adjacent content as MCC ships new capability (never ahead of real shipped features).
- First ROI tool: a real trial-to-value calculator tied to actual, disclosed pricing.

## Months 7-9

- Deepen highest-performing pillars (identified from real months 1-6 data) rather than spreading evenly — evidence-driven prioritization, not a fixed plan followed blindly.
- Begin cross-pillar comparison content (e.g., "governed AI vs. typical field-service software AI features") once enough real product-differentiation content exists to support it honestly.

## Months 10-12

- Fill remaining cluster gaps based on real search-query data (Search Console) rather than the original guess-based topic list.
- Annual content audit: update any piece with stale facts (pricing, product capability) — a real recurring task, not a one-time project.

## Publishing Cadence

Realistic pace: 2-4 pieces/month sustained, not a burst-then-abandon pattern. Quality and accuracy over volume — one honest, genuinely useful HVAC guide outperforms five thin ones, and per `BRAND_CONSTITUTION.md`, thin/fabricated content is not an option regardless of pace pressure.
