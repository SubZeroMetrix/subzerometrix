# Business Guidance Opportunity Map (Roadmap)

**Status:** Roadmap / documentation only. **Not built.** This maps the setup,
tracking, and checklist guidance SubZeroMetrix™ could provide to help contractors,
tradespeople, and service-business owners launch and operate correctly — and
identifies gaps not yet covered by the current roadmap.

Owner/operator: **The Modern Trades Mentor LLC**. Pairs with
`guided-business-foundation-builder.md` (Product-5).

**Guardrails (apply to every area below):** educational only; no legal/tax/financial/
licensing advice; route to official state/local/professional sources; no guarantee of
approval, licensing, leads, revenue, rankings, or success; no cloud-sync claim unless
built; no legal-compliance claim; no fake partner/affiliate claims; verify before
listing any vendor as a partner.

## Gaps identified vs current roadmap (candidates for Product-5)

The current roadmap covers assessment, score, roadmap, actions, tools, KPIs, sharing,
proof, partners, and analytics. The biggest **uncovered** guidance areas are:

- **Digital/technical setup** (A, F) — domains, hosting, email deliverability
  (SPF/DKIM/DMARC), account ownership, 2FA/password hygiene, backups. Not in the
  current product.
- **Step-by-step admin/legal/tax setup tracking** (B) — entity, EIN, registrations,
  registered agent, annual-report reminders — as *guided tracked steps* (not advice).
- **Financial setup worksheets** (C) — chart of accounts, receipt capture, startup
  budget, break-even, payroll readiness — beyond the existing manual KPI tracker.
- **Brand-profile completion tracking** (D) — a checklist across GBP + social +
  directories with completion status.
- **Operations templates** (E) — estimate/invoice/service-agreement/warranty/call
  scripts as tracked setup items.
- **Launch-execution sequencing** (G) — a structured pre-launch → soft-launch →
  30-day plan with completion tracking.
- **Bilingual setup** (H) and **compliance/trust setup** (J) as tracked foundation items.

These map directly into the Guided Business Foundation Builder categories — the
opportunity is to convert this guidance into **tracked, completable steps that move
measurable readiness**, closing the loop back to the MetrixScore™.
