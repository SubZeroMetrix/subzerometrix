## Backlink Strategy

Earned links only, driven by genuinely useful resource-center content (calculators, checklists, templates) that other sites want to reference — the same discipline as the content plan itself. No directory spam, no reciprocal-link schemes, no purchased placements.

## Social Distribution

Distribute real published content (guides, calculators, checklists) as it ships — no fabricated engagement, no purchased followers, no fake urgency in social copy, consistent with `BRAND_CONSTITUTION.md`'s marketing rules.

## Sequencing

All of the above should follow content, not precede it — per `CONTENT_CALENDAR.md`'s Launch Window and First 90 Days, there needs to be real, genuinely useful published content before outreach begins; reaching out with nothing real to show would waste the credibility this strategy depends on.
