## Content Brief Template

For each piece of content:

- **Target keyword**: Primary search term
- **Search intent**: Informational / Commercial / Transactional
- **Word count target**: 1,200–2,500 (reviews), 1,500–3,000 (comparisons), 800–1,500 (tutorials)
- **Internal links**: At least 3 relevant internal links per page
- **Affiliate CTA placement**: After introduction, after feature analysis, at conclusion
- **Affiliate disclosure**: Inline disclosure before first affiliate CTA
- **Schema type**: Product (reviews), Article (guides), BreadcrumbList (all), FAQ (where visible FAQs exist)
- **Author**: Richard Fritzke (link to /about/richard-fritzke)
- **Last reviewed date**: Publication date
- **Verification sources**: Vendor website, pricing page, documentation
- **Correction link**: Link to /contact for corrections

## Rules

- Do not fabricate reviews, user counts, test results, or statistics
- Do not copy vendor marketing language
- Clearly label editorial analysis vs verified vendor facts
- Include affiliate disclosure on every commercial page
- Include last-reviewed date
- Include correction/contact link
- Verify pricing against vendor sources before publishing
