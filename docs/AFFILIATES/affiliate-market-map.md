# Affiliate Market Map

## Purpose

Map the vendor and affiliate landscape for tradespeople starting or growing a contracting business. This informs the future vendor/affiliate marketplace feature.

## Categories

### Business Formation
- LLC filing services (e.g., ZenBusiness, Northwest Registered Agent)
- Business bank accounts (e.g., Relay, Bluevine)
- Accounting/bookkeeping (e.g., QuickBooks, FreshBooks)

### Insurance
- General liability insurance (e.g., Simply Business, Next Insurance)
- Workers comp
- Commercial auto

### Licensing & Compliance
- License exam prep (trade-specific)
- Bond providers
- State-specific compliance tools

### Tools & Equipment
- Tool financing
- Equipment rental platforms
- Supplier accounts (e.g., Home Depot Pro, Fastenal)

### Software & Operations
- Field service management (e.g., Jobber, Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan)
- Estimating software
- CRM tools

### Marketing & Leads
- Lead generation (e.g., Angi, Thumbtack, Google LSA)
- Website builders
- Reputation management

### Training & Certification
- Trade-specific certification programs
- Business skills courses

## Notes

- Prioritize programs with reliable affiliate payouts and contractor relevance.
- Track click and conversion data via the existing postback/tracking system.
- Do not list vendors without vetting for contractor fit.
